REVERSAL POLICY DOCUMENT
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. PURPOSE
This document defines the policy for reversing settled transactions in the ledger system.

2. REVERSAL WINDOWS
- Standard reversals: Allowed within 24 hours of settlement (T+0 to T+1)
- Extended reversals: Allowed within 48 hours for disputed transactions (T+0 to T+2)
- Emergency reversals: Allowed up to 72 hours with compliance approval (T+0 to T+3)

3. REVERSAL TYPES
- Full reversal: Complete reversal of the original transaction amount
- Partial reversal: Reversal of a portion of the original amount (requires approval)
- Chargeback reversal: Initiated by card network, follows network rules

4. APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
- Standard reversals (< 24h): Automated, no manual approval required
- Extended reversals (24-48h): Requires operations manager approval
- Emergency reversals (48-72h): Requires compliance officer approval + audit trail

5. REVERSAL PROCESS
1. Reversal request received with valid idempotency key
2. Original transaction validated (exists, not already reversed, within window)
3. Reversal transaction created with reversed entries (debit/credit swapped)
4. Original transaction marked as REVERSED with reversal_of reference
5. Ledger entries posted with same invariant checks
6. Event published to Kafka for downstream systems

6. COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS
- All reversals must maintain double-entry balance
- Reversal reason must be recorded in narrative field
- Audit log must capture: requester, approver, timestamp, original transaction
- Reversals after 48 hours require regulatory reporting within 24 hours

7. PROHIBITED REVERSALS
- Reversals beyond 72 hours without regulatory exemption
- Reversals of already reversed transactions
- Reversals that would violate account balance constraints (e.g., negative liability)

RECONCILIATION EXCEPTION HANDLING POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. PURPOSE
This document defines the policy for handling reconciliation exceptions identified during the three-way match process.

2. EXCEPTION TYPES
- MISSING_IN_LEDGER: Transaction exists in bank file but not in internal ledger
- MISSING_IN_BANK_FILE: Transaction exists in internal ledger but not in bank settlement
- AMOUNT_MISMATCH: Amount differs between ledger and bank (beyond tolerance)
- DUPLICATE: Duplicate transaction detected in bank file
- STATUS_MISMATCH: Transaction status differs between ledger and bank

3. TOLERANCE THRESHOLDS
- Amount tolerance: 0 minor units (exact match required for INR)
- FX transactions: 100 minor units tolerance for rounding differences
- Fee transactions: Fee amount tolerance as per fee schedule

4. EXCEPTION HANDLING PROCESS
1. Exception detected and written to reconciliation_exceptions table
2. Exception categorized by type and severity
3. Automated resolution attempted for known patterns
4. Unresolved exceptions queued for manual review
5. Resolution documented with corrective action
6. Exception marked as resolved with resolution details

5. ESCALATION MATRIX
- Level 1 (Operations): Standard exceptions, automated resolution
- Level 2 (Senior Operations): Complex exceptions, manual investigation
- Level 3 (Compliance): Regulatory impact exceptions, requires compliance sign-off

6. TIMELINES
- Detection to queue: Immediate (within reconciliation batch)
- Level 1 resolution: Within 4 business hours
- Level 2 resolution: Within 1 business day
- Level 3 resolution: Within 2 business days
- Regulatory reporting: As per RBI guidelines (typically T+1 for exceptions)

7. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
- Missing in ledger: Investigate webhook failure, re-post transaction
- Missing in bank: Verify settlement status with bank, may be pending
- Amount mismatch: Verify fees, FX rates, rounding; post adjustment if needed
- Duplicate: Identify root cause (retry storm, double webhook), reverse duplicate
- Status mismatch: Update status based on bank confirmation

8. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
- All exceptions must have resolution documented
- Root cause analysis required for exceptions > threshold
- Monthly exception trend report to management

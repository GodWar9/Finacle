## 3. Cloud-Native Architecture & Microservices

### 3.1 Cloud-Native Architecture (Current State)
**Claim:** Finacle solutions are built on a cloud-native, cloud-neutral architecture based on CNCF standards and the Twelve-factor App Methodology. The architecture includes microservices-based componentized design, stateless processes, Kubernetes orchestration, Istio service mesh, CI/CD pipelines, and support for deployment across AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, IBM, Oracle, and regional providers.

**Source:** Infosys Finacle Cloud Brochure (© 2026 EdgeVerve Systems Limited)  
**URL:** https://www.finacle.com/content/dam/infosys-finacle/pdf/solutions/infosys-finacle_on_cloud_brochure.pdf  
**Date:** 2026

**Key architecture tenets:**
- Microservices-based componentized architecture
- Stateless processes for agility and scalability
- Containerization with Kubernetes orchestration
- Service mesh (Istio)
- CI/CD pipelines
- Observability and analysis (Prometheus, Kibana)
- Streaming and messaging capabilities
- Declarative APIs and RESTful APIs

**Verification:** Verified (official Finacle PDF, © 2026)
